# Real-Time Retail Feedback Intelligence

## MIT Applied AI & Data Science – Capstone Project

### Project Overview

This project focuses on building a **Generative AI-powered retail feedback intelligence system** for ChicStyle, a fashion retail platform. The system transforms large volumes of unstructured customer reviews into actionable business insights by leveraging Generative AI and prompt engineering.

It aims to help retailers understand customer sentiment, identify product-specific feedback, detect urgent issues, and respond to customers more effectively, particularly during high-volume festive and holiday sales periods.

### Key Objectives

- **Sentiment Analysis:** Classify customer feedback as positive, negative, or neutral.
- **Product Identification:** Identify the products or services mentioned in customer reviews.
- **Urgency Detection:** Highlight critical customer concerns and high-impact issues.
- **Customer Engagement:** Generate personalized responses based on customer sentiment.
- **Business Intelligence:** Generate concise, actionable reports for retail teams.

### Generative AI Techniques

Explore and apply different prompt engineering approaches:
- Zero-Shot Prompting
- Few-Shot Prompting
- Chain-of-Thought Prompting

### Dataset

Women's E-Commerce Clothing Reviews dataset, containing customer reviews, ratings, product categories, recommendations, and feedback metrics.

### Expected Outcome

Convert unstructured customer feedback into meaningful, real-time business intelligence, enabling faster issue resolution, improved product quality, enhanced customer satisfaction, and stronger brand loyalty.
